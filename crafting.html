🤠 Server Guide / Crafting Reference

⚒️ Crafting Reference

All 14 professions, every recipe, ingredient requirements, and cross-profession dependencies.
Higher tiers require crafted materials from other professions — plan accordingly.

T1 Self-sourceable — mine, hunt, forage, farm it yourself
T2 Needs one other profession's output
T3 Requires 2–3 professions to cooperate
T4 Premium — depends on all major professions
⬡ Gold hex = cross-profession ingredient
